> Created by IBM Bob

## Core Sky Calm Palette

These colors are the foundation of all Calm Skies Travel materials — website, pitch deck, print, and social stories.

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| **Primary Background** | Cloud White | `#F7FBFF` | Page backgrounds, slide backgrounds, story pages |
| **Primary Brand** | Calm Sky Blue | `#5BA4CF` | Headlines, primary buttons, accents, slide header bars |
| **Deep Brand** | Clear Sky | `#2E7CB8` | Slide titles, active tab borders, strong CTA buttons |
| **Soft Accent** | Horizon Blue** | `#A8D4EF` | Subtle highlights, card borders, icon backgrounds |
| **Gentle Green** | Meadow Green | `#6BBF8E` | Success, checkmarks, calm indicators, go-bag icons |
| **Soft Green** | Soft Sage | `#B2DFC4` | Light card fills, child-facing section backgrounds |
| **Warm Neutral** | Warm Sand | `#F5EFE0` | Parent section backgrounds, warm contrast areas |
| **Text Primary** | Deep Slate | `#2D3748` | All body text, slide body content |
| **Text Muted** | Cool Gray | `#718096` | Captions, footnotes, secondary labels |
| **Pure White** | White | `#FFFFFF` | Text on dark fills, icon fills on colored backgrounds |

## Color Decisions Log

| Decision | Choice | Reason |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Primary blue | `#5BA4CF` (soft sky) not `#0066CC` (corporate) | Child-friendly; calm, not clinical |
| Green accent | `#6BBF8E` meadow green | Gentle, natural; used for "safe" and "calm" states |
| Background | `#F7FBFF` near-white with cool tint | Softer than pure white for sensory comfort |
| Warm Sand | `#F5EFE0` | Parent sections feel warmer; child sections stay sky-cool |
| Yellow | `#F9C74F` sunflower | Direct match to Sunflower Lanyard program branding |
| Heart red | `#F28B82` soft rose | Warm and loving without being alarming |
| IBM Blue | `#0F62FE` | IBM brand standard for the Bobathon badge only |

## Accessibility Notes

- All text on `#F7FBFF` backgrounds uses `#2D3748` (Deep Slate) — contrast ratio ≥ 7:1 ✅
- White text (`#FFFFFF`) on `#5BA4CF` — contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1 ✅ (meets WCAG AA)
- White text (`#FFFFFF`) on `#2E7CB8` — contrast ratio ≥ 7:1 ✅ (meets WCAG AAA)
- `#F9C74F` yellow — **never use as text color**; use as fill/icon only
- No reliance on color alone to convey meaning — always pair with icon or label

---

## Do Not Use

These colors are intentionally excluded from Calm Skies Travel:

| Color | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| Bright red `#FF0000` | Alarming; avoid in child-facing contexts |
| Neon / fluorescent colors | Sensory overload risk |
| Pure black `#000000` | Harsh on white; use Deep Slate `#2D3748` instead |
| Dark gray `#333333` on colored backgrounds | Check contrast before use |
