# Future Features — Calm Skies Travel
> Created by IBM Bob

This file captures features intentionally deferred from the initial build.
Revisit these decisions as the site matures and the user community grows.

---

## 1. Server-Side Child Profile Storage

**Current Decision:** No child data is ever stored on the server. All profile data lives in the parent's downloaded JSON file or QR code on their own device.

**Future Option:** An optional, privacy-first account system where parents can create a secure login and store their child's travel profile in the cloud. This would enable:
- Sync across devices (phone, tablet, laptop)
- Profile sharing with trusted caregivers, teachers, or relatives via a secure link
- Version history of past trips and social stories

**Considerations before revisiting:**
- COPPA and FERPA compliance (child data regulations)
- GDPR if site has European visitors
- Data minimization — collect only what is truly needed
- Opt-in only, never required
- Clear plain-language privacy policy written for parents, not lawyers

---

## 2. User Accounts & Community

**Future Option:** A private, moderated community space where parents and caregivers can share tips, customized social stories (with child details removed), and airport/destination reviews from an autism-friendly perspective.

---

## 3. Airport & Destination Sensory Reviews

**Future Option:** A crowd-sourced (moderated) directory of airports and destinations rated for sensory friendliness — noise levels, availability of quiet rooms, TSA Cares experience reports, etc.

---

## 4. Multi-Language Support

**Future Option:** Translate child-facing content and social stories into Spanish, French, and other languages to serve a broader community.

---

## 5. AAC / Communication Board Integration

**Future Option:** Export social stories in formats compatible with AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) devices and apps.

---

## 6. Therapist / School Collaboration Mode

**Future Option:** A print-ready "share with your therapist" export that includes the child's travel social story and go-bag list, formatted for clinical or school use.

---

## 7. Push Notifications / Flight Alerts

**Future Option:** Optional integration with flight status APIs so parents can receive calm, pre-written "your flight is delayed — here is what to tell your child" guidance automatically.
